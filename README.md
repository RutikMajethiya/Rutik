# Dhanya Bakers

A customer-focused bakery ordering web app concept for exploring bakery products and placing customized orders.

## About

Dhanya Bakers helps customers request cakes, cookies, bread, biscuits, cupcakes, and other baked delicacies for celebrations or everyday cravings. The ordering flow is designed to let customers choose a product category and specify their preferred flavour, shape, weight, colours, and design.

## Planned Features

- **Product selection:** Cakes, cookies, bread, biscuits, cupcakes, and a custom-requirement option.
- **Custom cake design:** Customers can upload a reference image or choose a design from a cake library.
- **Personalization:** Capture flavour, shape, weight, colours, and design preferences.
- **Custom requests:** Let customers describe a product they have in mind so the bakery can confirm whether it can be made.
- **Pricing rules:** Display applicable product prices, add-ons, and quantity-based discounts clearly.
- **Responsive experience:** Make the ordering flow convenient on desktop and mobile.

## Initial Product and Pricing Details

- **Bread:** ₹450 per kg for the standard recipe using butter, sunflower oil, and maida.
- **Flour substitutions:** Add ₹50 per kg when suji and/or whole-wheat flour is requested.
- **Cupcakes:** ₹50 each.
- **Cakes:** Planned quantity-based discounts: 10% on the additional kilogram up to 3 kg, and 5% on subsequent kilograms up to 5 kg, subject to the final pricing implementation.

Prices and discount calculations should be confirmed by the bakery before an order is finalized.
